Netflix & Spotify Streaming Analysis

This dashboard blends two entertainment datasets into a polished story of content release trends, audience behavior, and music characteristics. It is designed to guide viewers from an executive overview to deeper exploration through context-rich tooltips and interactive filters.

Netflix Dashboard - Page 1: Movie Overview

  • KPI Cards (5) — Count of Movies, Total Tmdb Score, Total Tmdb Popularity, Total Imdb Score, Count of Characters
  • Treemap — Movie Count by Age Certification (TV-Y7, TV-Y, TV-MA, PG, G, TV-PG, TV-14, PG-13, TV-G, R)
  • Pie/Donut Chart — Movie Count by Role (Actor vs Director split — 93.97% Actor, 6.03% Director)
  • Line Chart — Movie Count by Release Year (trend from ~2013 to 2022, peaking at 1026 in ~2021)
  • Slicer Panel — filters for Age Certification, Released Year, Type, Countries, Name, Imdb Score range, Tmdb Score range

Purpose: Gives a high-level snapshot of Netflix's movie catalog — how content is age-rated, who dominates the cast/crew roles, and how movie releases have trended over time.

Netflix Dashboard - Page 2: Release Trends & Global Reach

  • Bar Chart — Movie Count by Released Year (2014–2022)
  • Map (Bubble Map) — geographic distribution of movies by country, bubble size = count (North America and Europe dominate)
  • Table — Movie Title, Role, and Name (cast/crew list)

Purpose: Shows where Netflix content is produced/set and when it was released, with rich drill-down tooltips for deeper metrics without cluttering the main view.

Spotify Dashboard - Page 3: Track & Artist Analysis

  • KPI Cards (5) — Total Popularity, Total Duration (ms), Total Tempo, Total Time Signature, Total Mode
  • Bar Chart — Track Count by Artists (Top 10 artists by track count — The Beatles, George Jones, Stevie Wonder, etc.)
  • Line Chart — Total Tempo and Total Loudness by Album (dual-metric trend comparison across albums)
  • Table — Track Genre broken down by Danceability, Energy, and Liveness (with totals row)
  • Slicer Panel — filters for Album Name, Artists, Track Genre, Track Name, Tempo range, Popularity range

Purpose: Breaks down music characteristics (tempo, energy, danceability, loudness) by artist, album, and genre — useful for spotting which genres/artists trend toward high-energy vs mellow tracks.

Micro-views that enrich the dashboard

Tooltip 1 — Content type breakdown

Hovering a bar chart column reveals a mini stacked breakdown by content Type (Movie vs Show counts side-by-side).

Tooltip 2 — Geographic metric tooltip

Hovering a map bubble reveals Imdb Votes, Imdb Score, Tmdb Popularity, and Tmdb Score as an aggregated tooltip table.

Tooltip 3 — Title synopsis tooltip

Hovering a movie title in the table reveals its description/synopsis as a tooltip popup.

Tooltip 4 — Artist danceability tooltip

Hovering an artist bar reveals a ranked mini bar chart of Danceability by Track Genre for that artist's tracks.
